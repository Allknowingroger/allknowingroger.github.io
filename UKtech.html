Latest Highlights

Mindgard raises £22m

Lancaster University spinout Mindgard secured Series A funding to scale its AI security platform, which helps protect enterprise AI systems from emerging threats.

AI Bootcamps Launch

The UK government has launched AI training bootcamps for unemployed young people and a dedicated programme in the North West, aiming to tackle youth unemployment and build AI skills.

Economic Impact Visible

Official data shows the information and communications sector (including AI activity) driving a significant share of recent UK GDP growth, alongside rising investment in computing hardware.

Meta Glasses Ban

Courts in England and Wales have banned Meta smart glasses, reflecting growing attention to privacy and courtroom technology rules.

Funding & Startup Activity

AI companies dominated UK venture funding in the first half of 2026, accounting for the majority of mega-rounds and a large share of total capital raised.
  • Strong performance in enterprise software, health AI and infrastructure
  • Notable recent raises include Mindgard (AI security) and Cytix (AI code risk)
  • Continued activity in legal AI, deep tech and semiconductor-related startups
  • UK remains the top destination for venture capital in Europe

Government & Policy

Sovereign AI Fund

The UK’s Sovereign AI initiative continues to back domestic companies, supporting national capability in infrastructure, life sciences and AI assurance.

Skills & Adoption

Expanded AI training targets and bootcamps form part of a broader push to equip the workforce for the AI economy.

Hardware & Quantum

Significant public investment continues in AI hardware, compute capacity and quantum technologies to strengthen UK sovereignty in critical tech.

Key Themes in 2026

  • AI security & safety — Growing investment in tools that protect AI systems and code
  • Skills gap — Government and industry focus on rapid AI upskilling
  • Sovereign capability — Emphasis on domestic AI infrastructure and compute
  • Real-economy impact — AI beginning to show measurable contribution to GDP growth
  • Regulation & trust — Attention to privacy, courtroom tech and responsible AI use
