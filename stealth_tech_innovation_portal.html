Special Access Programs

Certain initiatives operate as Unacknowledged Acquisition SAPs. In these environments, the true name of the program is CLASSIFIED, and its funding is officially associated with an unrelated umbrella program to prevent adversary intelligence gathering and pattern analysis.

Document Markings

All physical and digital artifacts must display the highest overall classification or banner marking prominently. Furthermore, derivative classifiers must explicitly list their name, title, and organization within the designated AUTHORITY BLOCK to maintain the chain of custody.
