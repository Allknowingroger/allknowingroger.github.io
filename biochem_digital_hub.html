Historical Arc & Paradigm Shifts

The Molecular Miracle of Protein Structure

From John Kendrew extracting massive myoglobin crystals from Peruvian whale meat to the transformer-driven architectures of AlphaFold 2 and 3, predicting protein folding has shifted from decades of wet-lab crystallography to seconds of AI inference.

1958

Whale Myoglobin & Crystallography

John Kendrew struggled with small protein crystals until using myoglobin from deep-diving Peruvian whales. The resulting X-ray diffraction image looked intricate, irregular, and surprisingly asymmetrical—defying simple mathematical expectations.

First 3D protein structure solved
1994

CASP Competition & Monterey

John Moult founded CASP (Critical Assessment of Structure Prediction). Modelers received blind sequences to predict before experiment completion. At the Monterey chapel, foot-tapping signaled predictions that defied chemical sense!

Target score > 90 GDT = Solved
2008+

Foldit Gamer Crowdsourcing

David Baker created Foldit, turning protein folding into a video game. Over 50,000 players pooled human spatial intuition to twist protein backbones, deciphering retroviral protease enzymes in just three weeks where algorithms struggled.

Human spatial intuition unlocked
