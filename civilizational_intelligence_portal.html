Mathematical Foundations of Civilizational Free Energy

Rigorous information-theoretic equations governing multi-scale planetary active inference systems.

1. Variational Free Energy ($F$)
$F = \text{D}_{\text{KL}}\left[q(\theta) \parallel p(\theta)\right] - \mathbb{E}_{q}\left[\ln p(y \mid \theta)\right]$

Measures real-time dissonance between internal civilizational models $q(\theta)$ and environmental reality $y$. Minimizing $F$ guarantees accuracy while maintaining minimal model complexity.

2. Expected Free Energy ($G$)
$G(\pi) \approx - \text{Epistemic Value} - \text{Pragmatic Value}$

Evaluates candidate policy trajectories ($\pi$). Balances scientific exploration (reducing surprise/gaining knowledge) with engineering exploitation (securing survival resources).

3. Markov Blanket Condition
$\eta \in (\mu, b) \quad \text{where} \quad b = \{s, a\}$

Defines statistical independence between internal state ($\mu$) and external environment ($\eta$), conditional on sensory states ($s$) and active states ($a$).
